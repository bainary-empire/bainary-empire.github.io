Tab 1 · #renewal-form 1. Select Renewal Reason Upload Legal Declaration + Valid ID, then show how the reason dropdown reveals the exact extra proof needed.Show inline Valid IDs & Requirements FAQ link
4 Renewal Reasons
Expiring / Expired Core declaration & Valid ID only. No penalty.
Passport is Lost Requires Affidavit of Loss upload (+₱350 penalty).
Passport is Damaged Requires Damaged / Affidavit upload (+₱350 penalty).
Citizen Status Changed Requires Status Change Certificate upload.
Step 2 of 5 2. Personal + DFA Office Prefilled account details, current passport info, and DFA Consular Office placed right beside Passport Place of Issue.
Step 3 of 5 3. Contact & Emergency Present/permanent address, mobile, occupation, and emergency contact.
Step 4 of 5 4. Sworn Oath & Signature Accept RA 8239 sworn declaration and enter electronic signature.
Step 5 of 5 5. Live Fee & Submit Renewal Shows Processing + Courier + ₱350 penalty total and full review grid → creates BAI-REN record in Pending state.
